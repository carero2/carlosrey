Carlos Rey

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Marketing Department at ESADE, funded by a Ramón Areces Postdoctoral Fellowship. I study how people interact with large language models (learning, user experience and trust) and how they make decisions under losses, combining behavioral experiments, cognitive modelling, NLP and machine learning.

I hold a PhD in Management (Decision Sciences) from the Universitat de les Illes Balears and an MSc in Data Science.
